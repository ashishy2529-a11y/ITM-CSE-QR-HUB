# Student Guidelines — B.Tech CSE Semester III

## Attendance and punctuality
- Check the official timetable regularly and arrive before each class begins.
- Track attendance after every lecture and practical. Contact the faculty/department early if attendance is at risk.
- Keep an eye on the three attendance review dates in the academic calendar.

## Study routine
- Maintain separate unit-wise notes for Numerical Methods, Java, DBMS, Probability & Statistics, and Frontend Design with React.
- Revise each lecture on the same day. Keep a formula sheet for Numerical Methods and Probability.
- Practise numerical problems step by step; do not rely only on memorising final formulas.
- Write and run Java programs regularly. Practise SQL queries, joins, grouping, constraints and normalization.
- For React, practise components, props, state, hooks, routing and API integration by building small working examples.

## Tests, practicals and assignments
- Record class tests, practicals, assignment deadlines and examination dates in one calendar.
- Attend labs prepared. Keep practical records, source code, screenshots and viva questions organized.
- Submit original work. Acknowledge books, websites, libraries and other sources as required by university rules.

## Digital safety
- Never share passwords, OTPs or private academic records through public QR codes.
- Before scanning a QR code, check that its destination is the expected university/project resource.
- Do not enter credentials on an unfamiliar page or download unexpected files.

## Examination preparation
- Revise unit by unit and practise previous questions where available.
- Prepare definitions, algorithms, formulae, worked examples and short programs.
- Follow faculty instructions and the latest official university notices. This resource is a student aid, not a replacement for official circulars.
